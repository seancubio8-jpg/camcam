Technical Skills • Troubleshooting

My Problem-Solving Approach

How I identify challenges, understand their causes, test solutions, and improve my work.

🔍

1. Identify the Problem

When I encounter a problem in a website, database, or hardware project, I observe what is happening and compare it with the expected result. I check error messages and identify which part may be responsible.

Example: If an order does not appear, I check form fields, application logic, the database connection, and saved records.
🛠️

2. Test Possible Solutions

I test solutions one at a time. I review code, verify database queries, check input values, and test different scenarios. I avoid changing many parts at once because errors become harder to trace.

My approach: Make a small change, test the result, and record what worked.
💡

3. Evaluate and Improve

Once a solution works, I test related features to make sure the fix does not create another problem. I also think about how the issue happened and how to prevent it next time.

What I learned: Patience, careful testing, logical thinking, and willingness to learn are important.

My Reflection

What I have learned from my academic journey, project development, and workplace preparation.

📚

Learning Through Experience

As an IT student, I have learned that understanding a lesson is different from applying it to a project. Building websites, working with databases, and exploring Arduino have helped me understand how technical concepts work together.

These experiences have taught me to be patient and continue practicing when a feature does not work on the first attempt.

🚀

Challenges and Improvements

One challenge in project development is finding errors and making sure different parts of a system work properly. The interface, program logic, or database sometimes needs to be checked several times.

I have learned to break large tasks into smaller steps, test regularly, and ask for feedback when needed.

🌟

Preparing for the Future

Creating this portfolio has helped me organize my projects and present the skills I am developing. It has reminded me that learning does not stop after completing a school requirement.

I want to improve my programming, communication, teamwork, and time-management skills.
